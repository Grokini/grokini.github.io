FAQ

Common questions

What is RSI and why does it matter for options? +
RSI (Relative Strength Index) measures whether a stock is overbought or oversold based on recent price movements. Extreme RSI readings — especially on the weekly chart — historically precede significant price reversals, which is exactly what options traders want to position ahead of.
How many alerts will I get per day? +
Typically 5-15 alerts per day depending on market conditions. Volatile markets produce more extreme RSI readings and therefore more alerts. Each stock can only alert once per day so you never get spammed with the same ticker repeatedly.
Do I need trading experience to use these alerts? +
Basic options knowledge is helpful — understanding calls, puts, and the concept of IV will help you act on alerts effectively. Every alert includes a plain-language strategy recommendation so even newer traders know which direction and approach to consider.
